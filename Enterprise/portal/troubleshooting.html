    Report a problem to IT

    Choose an assigned device and use the problem and safety selections above. Consent permits recording a report only; no diagnostic collection or repair.

    Employee report assignment

    Use the exact device ID and employee user ID from the administration portal. Confirm that this employee is authorized to report on this device. This grants report-only access, not diagnostic or repair authority.

    For emergencies, stop using the affected device and seek qualified assistance. Contact your organization's IT team for authorized diagnostics and repairs.
